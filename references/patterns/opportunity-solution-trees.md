# Opportunity-Solution Trees

## Summary

Opportunity-Solution Trees (Teresa Torres) are a discovery artefact for continuous product discovery: a desired outcome at the top, decomposed into opportunities (customer needs or pain points), then candidate solutions, then assumption tests for each solution. The tree is the artefact of a discovery practice, not a one-time deliverable.

## When to use

- Discovery work in any domain that has a "customer" (internal or external)
- Decomposing a strategic outcome into testable assumptions
- Communicating discovery progress without overcommitting to specific solutions

## Canonical source

Teresa Torres, "Continuous Discovery Habits" — <https://www.producttalk.org/>

## Note on use in OrganisationOS

Outside product organisations, the "customer" may be an internal stakeholder, a peer department, a client, or the public. The tree shape works in any discovery context, not just consumer software.
