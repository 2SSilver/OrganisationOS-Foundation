# Wardley Mapping

## Summary

Wardley Mapping is a strategic-planning technique that places a value chain on two axes — visibility-to-user (vertical) and stage-of-evolution (horizontal: genesis → custom → product → commodity). The map surfaces where to invest, where to outsource, and where competitors will likely move next.

## When to use

- Diagnosing a domain's portfolio of activities and where each sits on the evolution axis
- Anticipating commoditisation in a part of the business
- Communicating strategy visually to non-engineers in a single image
- Pairing with Cynefin or Cynefin-adjacent decision frameworks

## Canonical source

Simon Wardley, "Wardley Maps" — https://learnwardleymapping.com/ and the book serialised on Medium.

## Note on use in OrganisationOS

A common failure mode is mistaking the map for the diagnostic. The honest framing is "build the diagnostic, then retire the map" — the map is the discovery output; the codified diagnostic that comes from reading the map is the reusable IP.
